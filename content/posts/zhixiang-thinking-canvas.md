I kept hitting the same wall with chat-based AI. Digging into a hard topic — a paper, an architecture decision, an investment thesis — the conversation would scroll, the early derivations would slip out of context, and a side question I wanted to chase would either die in the scrollback or derail the whole thread. And after all of it, I could rarely point at where a claim came from.

Deep thinking is not a single line of dialogue. It branches, backtracks, and rearranges. So I built [Zhixiang (枝想)](https://kg.hao430.cn/) — an infinite-canvas thinking space that treats a conversation with AI the way it actually happens: as a map, not a transcript.

## Branch on anything you select

While reading a long answer, select any term, formula, or claim you want to chase. A floating capsule appears and grows that selection into an independent sub-branch — with the surrounding context automatically inherited, so the new thread knows what it is a question about. You can keep recursing: every branch can spawn its own branches, until the topic is fully unpacked on one board.

## Sources you can actually click

The tool runs real-time web retrieval and pulls in authoritative papers, official docs, and industry reports. Every claim carries citation badges, and each badge opens the source card right on the canvas. The goal is simple: nothing you build your thinking on should be unverifiable.

## Math and code are first-class citizens

LaTeX renders inline and as display equations — matrices, partial derivatives, integrals — so formal reasoning survives the round trip. Code gets syntax highlighting, and the chain of thought behind an answer can be collapsed open for review instead of being a black box.

## Your hypotheses beside the AI's reasoning

A "dual-track" note system lets you drop your own research assumptions onto the same canvas where the AI is reasoning, so your prior and its argument grow in one graph instead of living in separate apps. When a session ends, export the whole thing as a structured Markdown report, or back it up as JSON. There is a night-mode canvas for late-night sessions.

## No account. Your canvas lives in your browser

Zhixiang does not ask for an account or phone number. Open the page and start; progress auto-saves to your browser, which also means your research data does not leave your machine unless you export it.

Whether it is working through a paper theorem by theorem, weighing an architecture trade-off, stress-testing an investment thesis, or building a mental model of a new field — I built this for the kind of thinking that refuses to fit in a single chat window.

Open [kg.hao430.cn](https://kg.hao430.cn/) and start branching. Feedback is welcome — it is early, and the map is still being drawn.
